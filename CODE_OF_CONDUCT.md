# Community expectations

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, lock discussions,
or restrict participation. Moderation should be proportionate and explain the
behavior at issue when doing so is safe.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for harassment or
private-information exposure. Report software vulnerabilities through the private
channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not use public issues to disclose
sensitive details about other participants.
